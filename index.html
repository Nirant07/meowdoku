Meowdoku

Place one cat in every row, column, and colour region. Cats cannot touch — not even diagonally.

Easy 0 moves

Rules

  • Exactly one cat in each row.
  • Exactly one cat in each column.
  • Exactly one cat in each connected colour region.
  • Cats may not touch horizontally, vertically, or diagonally.
Choose a cell to place a cat.
Click cells to place or remove cats. A new puzzle is generated locally in your browser.
